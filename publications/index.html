Publications

Publications in reverse chronological order.

2026

  1. COLM
    Closing the Confidence-Faithfulness Gap in Large Language Models
    Miranda Muqing Miao, and Lyle Ungar
    Under Review at COLM 2026, 2026
    Abs Bib HTML

    A mechanistic interpretability paper on verbalized confidence faithfulness, showing that calibration and confidence signals are linearly encoded but orthogonal across three models. Identified the "Reasoning Contamination Effect" and introduced a two-stage adaptive steering pipeline that substantially improves calibration alignment.

  2. ICML
    Correctness-Optimized Residual Activation Lens (CORAL): Transferrable and Calibration-Aware Inference-Time Steering
    Miranda Muqing Miao, Young-Min Cho, and Lyle Ungar
    ICML 2026, 2026
    5.0 Avg. Rating, 3.33 Avg. Confidence, Accept
    Abs Bib HTML

    A mechanistic interpretability approach to inference-time steering that extracts distributed correctness signals from frozen LLM residual stream activations via auxiliary probes, improving accuracy and calibration with cross-task generalization.

  3. arXiv
    Contrastive Conceptor Activation Steering (COAST): Unlocking Vision-Language-Action Models through Hidden States
    Miranda Muqing Miao, Subin Kim, Brandon Yang, and 1 more author
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.17144, 2026
    Abs Bib HTML

    Introduces COAST, which uses conceptors—linear operators projecting data into principal component subspaces—to identify success-critical activation patterns in vision-language-action models, improving simulation and real-robot task success rates by over 20% and 40% across multiple VLA architectures without retraining.

  4. arXiv
    Conceptors for Semantic Steering
    Ilias Triantafyllopoulos, Young-Min Cho, Ren Tao, and 6 more authors
    arXiv preprint arXiv:2605.04980, 2026
    Abs Bib HTML

    Introduces conceptors—soft projection matrices derived from activations—for controlling language model behavior. Unlike single-direction steering, conceptors preserve a concept’s full multidimensional subspace, outperforming prior steering methods across multiple models and semantic dimensions.

  5. PNAS
    Hallucination, Monofacts, and Miscalibration: An Empirical Investigation
    Miranda Muqing Miao, and Michael Kearns
    Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS), 2026
    Abs Bib HTML

    The first empirical validation of the theoretical link between LLM hallucination and training data monofact rates, demonstrating controllable hallucination through data distribution manipulation and introducing selective upweighting for mitigation.

2025

  1. EMNLP
    The Impact of Language Mixing on Bilingual LLM Reasoning
    Yihao Li, Jiayi Xin, Miranda Muqing Miao, and 2 more authors
    In EMNLP 2025 Main, 2025
    Abs Bib HTML

    Identifies RLVR as the training stage causing language mixing in bilingual reasoning models, showing that mixing enhances mathematical accuracy and developing a lightweight probe to guide beneficial code-switching during decoding.

  2. ACL
    Multilingual Retrieval Augmented Generation for Culturally-Sensitive Tasks: A Benchmark for Cross-lingual Robustness
    Bryan Li, Fiona Luo, Samar Haider, and 7 more authors
    In ACL 2025 Findings, 2025
    Abs Bib HTML

    Contributes to BordIRLines, a multilingual RAG benchmark for territorial disputes across 49 languages, demonstrating that cross-lingual retrieval improves response consistency and reduces geopolitical bias compared to monolingual approaches.
