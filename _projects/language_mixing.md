This work identifies RLVR as the training stage causing language mixing in bilingual reasoning models, showing that mixing enhances mathematical accuracy. We develop a lightweight probe to guide beneficial code-switching during decoding.

**Published**: EMNLP 2025 Main

**Authors**: Yihao Li, Jiayi Xin, Miranda Muqing Miao, Qi Long, Lyle Ungar

[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15849){: .btn}
