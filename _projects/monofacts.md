The first empirical validation of the theoretical link between LLM hallucination and training data monofact rates. We demonstrate controllable hallucination through data distribution manipulation and introduce selective upweighting for mitigation.

Key contributions:
- Empirical validation that monofact rates in training data predict hallucination rates
- Controllable hallucination: manipulating data distributions systematically changes model hallucination behavior
- Selective upweighting as a practical mitigation strategy

**Published**: PNAS 2025

**Authors**: Miranda Muqing Miao, Michael Kearns
