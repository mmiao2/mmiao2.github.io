Research

My research sits at the intersection of mechanistic interpretability, LLM calibration, and AI safety. I build methods that peer inside language models to understand and steer their internal representations of confidence, correctness, and faithfulness.

Interpretability & Steering

CORAL: Inference-Time Steering via Residual Activations

Extracting distributed correctness signals from frozen LLM residual streams for calibration-aware, transferable steering at inference time.

Closing the Confidence-Faithfulness Gap

Mechanistic analysis showing calibration and confidence signals are linearly encoded but orthogonal, with adaptive steering to align them.

Calibration & Truthfulness

Hallucination, Monofacts, and Miscalibration

First empirical validation linking LLM hallucination rates to training data monofact distributions, with controllable mitigation.

Multilingual NLP

Language Mixing in Bilingual LLM Reasoning

Identifying RLVR as the training stage causing language mixing in bilingual reasoning models, with a lightweight probe for guided code-switching.

BordIRLines: Multilingual RAG for Culturally-Sensitive Tasks

A multilingual RAG benchmark for territorial disputes across 49 languages, showing cross-lingual retrieval reduces geopolitical bias.

Ongoing

Conceptors for Semantic Steering

Using conceptor-based methods for fine-grained semantic control of language model representations.

VLA Policy Steering

Applying activation steering methods to Vision-Language-Action models for improved robotic policy alignment and safety.

CollabWithPeers

A platform for facilitating research collaboration among PhD students and early-career researchers.
