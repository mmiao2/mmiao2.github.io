About

CS Master's student at the University of Pennsylvania

I'm Miranda Muqing Miao, a master's student in Computer & Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania. I study large language models with a focus on truthfulness, calibration, and multilingualism. I'm part of the Kearns–Roth Research Group and the Penn NLP Lab at the University of Pennsylvania.
