About

Miranda Miao – Master's student at UPenn and graduate from Wellesley College researching LLMs, papers, and more.

I'm Miranda Muqing Miao, a master's student in Computer & Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania. I study large language models with a focus on truthfulness, calibration, and retrieval. I'm part of the Kearns–Roth Research Group and the Penn NLP Lab at the University of Pennsylvania.
